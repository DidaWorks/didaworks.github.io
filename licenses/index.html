Course Workload Estimator: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

The Course Workload Estimator is an adaptation of the Course Workload Estimator by Betsy Barre, Allen Brown, and Justin Esarey (Rice University Center for Teaching Excellence; Workload Estimator 2.0, Wake Forest University Center for the Advancement of Teaching). The original is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License, and so is this adaptation. It is the one DidaWorks page that is not under the MIT License.

Changes from the original: reading and writing rates are taken from the original Estimation Details; added a configurable credit-hour benchmark and class-hour length, term-length presets, labeled default assumptions for media, discussion posts, and exams, a copyable summary, and new visual design.

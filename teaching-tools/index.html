Teaching tools

Free tools for the repetitive parts of teaching: course planning, exams, and clinical teaching.

Small, free tools for the repetitive parts of teaching. Each one runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere, and there's no account to make.

Tools

Course Workload Estimator

Estimate how many hours students spend on a course, per week and per term, and compare the total with a credit-hour benchmark.

  • Reading, writing, media, discussion, exams, class time
  • Your institution's hours-per-credit and class-hour length
Adapted from Barre, Brown & Esarey · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

Syllabus Date Shifter

Roll a syllabus or course schedule forward to a new term. Dates move by course week and weekday.

  • Word files keep their formatting
  • Handles break weeks and no-class days
  • Review every change before downloading
MIT License

Nursing Exam Tools

Build and review teacher-made nursing exams.

  • Item analysis helper
  • Test blueprint builder
  • Exam timing calculator
  • NGN unfolding case builder
For nursing faculty · MIT License

Nursing Clinical Teaching Tools

Speed up the clinical side of a nursing course.

  • Dosage-calculation problem generator
  • Clinical and simulation hours tracker
  • Rotation grid builder
  • Clinical feedback comment bank
For nursing faculty · MIT License

DidaCommons™

The community space around DidaWorks™: ideas, fixes, templates, and help, shared in the open.

DidaWorks tools get better when the people who teach with them say what they need. DidaCommons is where that happens. It runs on GitHub Discussions, so it's free, public, and searchable.

What you can do there

Suggest a feature

Tell us what would save you time. Say what you're trying to do, not just the button you'd add.

Report what broke

Name the tool, the browser, and what you expected to happen. Never post student data or files.

Share a template

Rubric comment banks, test blueprints, workload presets: anything another instructor could reuse.

Ask a question

Not sure a tool fits your course? Ask. Someone has probably tried it.

Go to the discussions

Posting needs a free GitHub account. Reading doesn't. Everything you post is public, so keep student names, grades, and course files out of it.
